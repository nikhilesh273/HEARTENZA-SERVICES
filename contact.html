Why are prices not shown on the website?

Each request varies by location, timing and scope. We understand the details first, then share a personalised quote directly before you confirm.

Can I arrange help for someone else?

Yes. You can enquire for a parent or loved one in Wayanad while living elsewhere. We will clarify consent, communication and practical requirements.

Can one enquiry include more than one service?

Yes. Select the closest service and explain the complete requirement in your message.

Is submitting the form a confirmed booking?

No. It starts an enquiry. A service is confirmed only after availability, scope, timing and the personalised quote have been agreed.

Does medical assistance cover emergencies?

No. This is non-emergency, non-clinical assistance and does not replace doctors, nurses, ambulances or emergency services.
