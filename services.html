Still have a question? We are happy to talk it through.

How do I enquire about a service?

Choose “View service” to read more, or “Enquire” to open WhatsApp with that service already mentioned. Share your location, date and requirements. Our team will discuss the next steps with you.

How will I know the price?

We share a personalised quote directly after understanding your request. There are no fixed prices displayed here. You can review the details with our team before confirming the service.

Can I arrange help for someone else?

Yes. You can enquire on behalf of a parent or loved one in Wayanad, even if you live elsewhere. We will discuss the support needed, their consent and how to keep your family informed.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Tell us everything you need, such as a driver and hospital-visit assistance, or home upkeep and a delivery. Our team will check availability and discuss how the services can be coordinated.

Is medical assistance an emergency service?

No. Our medical assistance is non-emergency, non-clinical coordination for appointments, hospital visits and medicine collection. It does not replace a doctor, nursing care or emergency services. For an emergency, contact your local emergency services directly.
