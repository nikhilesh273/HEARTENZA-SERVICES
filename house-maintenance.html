What kinds of home tasks can I request?

You can enquire about plumbing, electrical work, cleaning and everyday home repairs. Describe the exact issue so we can clarify what support is possible.

Do you provide garden and outdoor services?

Yes. We provide garden maintenance, grass cutting, planting and plantation work, paddy field work, and general outdoor cleaning and maintenance. Tell us the location and approximate area or scope.

Can I arrange help for a home while living away?

Yes. Share the property location, who can provide access and how you would like updates. We will discuss what can be coordinated before confirmation.

Do I need to send photos?

Photos can make the issue easier to understand, but you can begin with a written description. For outdoor work, an approximate area is helpful too. Some jobs may still need an on-site assessment.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The scope, access, timing, area size, materials and any specialist work vary. We discuss a personalised quote directly before you confirm.

Can I use this for an urgent hazard?

This is an enquiry-led maintenance service, not an emergency-response line. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency or qualified utility service first.
