What does elderly companion support include?

It can begin with a companion visit, everyday errand, planned outing or practical non-clinical help. We discuss the exact support and availability before confirmation.

Can I arrange help while I live away?

Yes. Share your loved one's location, support request, access contact and how you would like updates. We discuss what can be arranged.

Is this medical or emergency care?

No. This is non-clinical companion support, not nursing, ambulance or emergency care. For an urgent health situation, contact emergency medical services or a qualified healthcare provider.

Why is there no fixed price?

Visit length, task, place, timing and support required vary. We discuss a personalised quote directly before you confirm.
