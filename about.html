What Heartenza is.
And what it isn’t.

Clarity builds trust. We want customers to understand how the platform works before sending a request.

Heartenza is

  • A local coordination point for multiple services
  • A personalised enquiry and confirmation process
  • A way to arrange help for yourself or loved ones
  • A team that discusses scope and availability first

Heartenza is not

  • An instant checkout or fixed-price marketplace
  • A guarantee that every request is immediately available
  • A substitute for doctors, nurses or emergency services
  • A confirmed booking until details are agreed

What kind of company is Heartenza?

Heartenza is a local multi-service platform based in Wayanad. We help coordinate travel, driver, home, vehicle, delivery, companion, medical-assistance and occasion-related requests.

Can I arrange support while living elsewhere?

Yes. You can discuss assistance for parents or loved ones in Wayanad even when you live in another city or abroad. We will clarify consent, communication and practical requirements first.

Why is there no pricing on the website?

Requirements vary by service, location, timing and scope. We first understand what you need, then share a personalised quote directly before confirmation.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Tell us the complete situation and we can discuss how related requests may be coordinated, subject to availability.

Does Heartenza provide emergency or clinical care?

No. Medical assistance is non-emergency and non-clinical. It does not replace a doctor, nurse, ambulance or emergency service.
