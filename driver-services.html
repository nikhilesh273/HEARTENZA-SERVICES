Clear expectations

Know what you are
requesting.

Good coordination starts with a shared understanding of the service.

This service is for

  • A temporary driver for your suitable vehicle
  • Agreed local or planned outstation routes
  • Journeys arranged around a date and expected timing
  • Requests confirmed after availability is checked

Please keep in mind

  • This is not presented as a taxi or vehicle-rental service
  • The vehicle must be roadworthy and appropriately documented
  • Fuel, toll, parking and overnight details are discussed as relevant
  • This service is not emergency medical transport

Does the service include a car?

This page is for requesting a driver for your own suitable vehicle. If your requirement is different, describe it in your enquiry and we will clarify what may be available.

Can I request an outstation driver?

Planned outstation journeys can be discussed. Availability depends on the route, duration, dates and pickup location.

Can I request a driver for only a few hours?

Share the required reporting time, approximate duration and route. We will check whether a suitable arrangement is available.

Why is there no fixed price online?

The price depends on the route, hours, journey type and other practical requirements. We share a personalised quote directly before confirmation.

Is sending an enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. Your request is confirmed only after the details, availability and quote are agreed with you.
