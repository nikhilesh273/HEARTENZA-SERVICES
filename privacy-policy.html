1. Who we are

Heartenza Services (“Heartenza”, “we”, “us”) coordinates local travel, home, vehicle, family and everyday support services in Wayanad, Kerala, India. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Heartenza Services is the data fiduciary (the organisation responsible for your personal data).

Our registered correspondence address is Building No 407/B, Kammana P.O., Mananthavady, Wayanad, Kerala. You can reach us at heartenzaservices@gmail.com or +91 81118 44058.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information needed to understand and coordinate your request. Depending on how you contact us and which service you choose, this may include:

  • Details you give us — your name, phone number, optional email address, service location, preferred date and the message you send us.
  • Service-specific details — for example a route and pickup point for a driver, a property address for a home or CCTV request, or recipient details for a delivery or gift.
  • Information about others — when you arrange a service for a family member or loved one, you may share their details. We ask that you do so with their knowledge and consent.
  • Technical information — when you browse our website, standard technical data such as your device type, browser and pages viewed may be processed automatically by our website and analytics tools.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — as described in section 6.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as payment card numbers, government identification numbers or medical records through this website.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss the details of your request.
  • Check availability and share a personalised quote.
  • Coordinate and arrange the service you confirm, including briefing the person carrying it out.
  • Keep you informed about the status of a request.
  • Improve our services, website and the clarity of our communication.
  • Meet legal, accounting and safety obligations where they apply.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the service you asked about without telling you.

4. Consent & legal basis

We rely on your consent when you send an enquiry or agree to a service. By contacting us, you consent to us using the details you provide to respond to your request. Where you share information about another person, you confirm that you have their permission to do so.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer continue coordinating a service, but it will not affect anything already done with your permission.

5. WhatsApp & enquiry forms

Our website enquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to our team directly. When you use it, the details you entered are shared through WhatsApp, and your use of WhatsApp is also governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. We recommend you review their terms before sending sensitive information.

You can also reach us by phone or email. Wherever you contact us, we handle the information you share in the same way described in this policy.

6. Cookies & Meta Pixel

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. We also use the Meta Pixel, which helps measure the performance of our advertising and understand how visitors reach us. These tools may set cookies and process technical information about your visit.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and many browsers let you block third-party cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

7. Third-party services we rely on

To run our website and services, we rely on trusted third parties. These may process limited information on our behalf:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — for enquiries and communication.
  • Meta Pixel — for advertising measurement.
  • Google Fonts — for typography used on this website.
  • Google Maps — to show our location on the contact page.
  • Cloudinary — for hosting some travel images.
  • Web hosting and analytics providers — to serve the website and understand its use.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review it. We choose providers that are widely used and reputable, but we do not control how they handle information outside our instructions.

8. How we share information

We share your information only as far as needed to arrange your service. This usually means passing the relevant details to the driver, worker, companion, technician or partner who will carry out the request. For example, a driver receives the pickup point, route and timing, while a delivery contact receives the pickup and drop addresses.

We may also share information where we are legally required to do so, or to protect the safety of our team, our customers or the public. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

9. How long we keep information

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to coordinate your request, follow up afterwards, and meet any legal or accounting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

10. Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need.
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your information.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you for non-essential purposes.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 16. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

12. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, including limiting access to it and handling it carefully. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your information safe and to respond promptly if an issue arises.

13. International transfers

Some of the third-party providers we rely on, such as global messaging, hosting and analytics platforms, may store or process information outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to be protected.
