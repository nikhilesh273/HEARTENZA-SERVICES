A few helpful answers

Wondering
about something?

Can I arrange help for family while I’m away?

Yes. Tell us where your family is in Wayanad and what they need. Our team will discuss availability, arrangements and how to keep you informed.

How do I get a price for a service?

Send an enquiry with your requirements. We’ll discuss the details and share a personalised quote directly with you before arranging the service.

Is my enquiry a confirmed booking?

Your enquiry starts a conversation. Our team confirms availability, scope and the price with you first. A service is arranged after you agree.

What does medical assistance include?

Practical, non-emergency help with appointments, hospital visits, medicine collection and transport coordination. This service does not provide medical treatment.
