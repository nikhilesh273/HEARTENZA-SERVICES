Is this a fixed itinerary?

No. The listed attractions are ideas for your enquiry. The final route depends on your dates, available time, interests and current local access.

How will I know the price?

Share your group, dates and requirements. We will discuss the itinerary and provide a personalised quote directly before you confirm.

Can I combine this area with another?

Yes. We can discuss places from other Wayanad areas and help create a practical route based on travel time and the number of days available.

Does selecting a place confirm a booking?

No. Selecting places only adds them to your enquiry message. Nothing is booked until you discuss the plan and confirm it with our team.

Can access to an attraction change?

Yes. Weather, permits, maintenance and local operating conditions can affect access. Our team will help check practical details during planning.
