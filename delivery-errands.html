Can you send a parcel from Wayanad to another country?

Yes, we can discuss international parcel delivery to available countries. Share the country, contents and approximate size or weight so the route and requirements can be checked.

Can I arrange it while I live abroad?

Yes. You can enquire from anywhere and coordinate an eligible parcel or practical local request for a family member in Wayanad. We discuss collection, recipient and update details before confirmation.

Can you deliver medicines locally?

Non-urgent medicine collection and delivery can be requested where available. The original valid prescription is required when the medicine requires one. This is not an emergency delivery service.

What cannot be sent?

Availability depends on destination and carrier requirements. Do not send prohibited, unsafe or undisclosed contents. Tell us exactly what the parcel contains so we can discuss whether it can be accepted.

Why is there no fixed delivery price?

International cost depends on destination, weight, size, contents and carrier requirements. Local delivery depends on task, distance and timing. We share a personalised quote directly before confirmation.
