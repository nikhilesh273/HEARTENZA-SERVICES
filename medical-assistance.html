Clear and safe expectations

Support around care.
Never a replacement for it.

We make the role of this service clear, so families can make the right choice for their loved one.

This service can start with

  • A planned hospital, clinic or diagnostic appointment
  • Practical accompaniment or coordination request
  • Clear family, access and emergency-contact details
  • An agreed way to share practical updates

Please keep in mind

  • This is not nursing, diagnosis or medical treatment
  • It is not an ambulance or emergency medical service
  • For urgent symptoms, call emergency medical services immediately
  • A healthcare professional remains responsible for clinical decisions

What does medical visit assistance include?

It is practical, non-clinical support around a planned hospital or clinic visit, such as accompaniment, appointment coordination and agreed family updates. We confirm the exact scope after enquiry.

Can I arrange it while living away?

Yes. Share the appointment place, timing, practical support needed and emergency contact. We will discuss what non-clinical support is possible.

Is this emergency medical care?

No. This service does not provide diagnosis, treatment, nursing, ambulance or emergency care. For an emergency, call local emergency medical services immediately.

Why is there no fixed price?

Appointment location, timing, visit length and practical support vary. We discuss a personalised quote directly before confirmation.
